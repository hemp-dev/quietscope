# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful, safe, and welcoming.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be precise about safety and privacy concerns.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep security reports responsible and do not publish other people's secrets.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior is unsafe, abusive, or disruptive. Security and privacy violations are handled with extra care.
